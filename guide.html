Race rules

  • Fly through every gate and checkpoint, then cross the finish line. Finish time decides the winner.
  • Hitting an obstacle, missing a gate or leaving the course causes a crash. You return to your last checkpoint.
  • Practice is solo. Online races support 2–8 players.
  • Barrel rolls and clean gates earn style points. Style does not change your finish time or Flap Points.

Pitch aims your bird; flapping provides lift. Roll through narrow twist slots. Diving trades height for speed. Touch controls support multiple fingers.

Courses

Three courses per difficulty. Each course in a tier is harder than the previous one.

Boost and pickups

Gold crystals refill boost. Blue crystals protect against frame scrapes for 4 seconds. Shields cannot bypass a missed opening or the course boundaries.

Race events

Warnings appear before each shared event: Tailwind increases speed, Low Gravity reduces falling, and Gusts add crosswind.

Flap Points and the shop

    Unused points carry to halfway. Points and equipment reset each race. Permanent upgrades do not stack. You can buy a spent Guard or Jammer charge again at halfway.

    Items

    Flap Shop prices and effects
    ItemCostEffect

    Items work automatically. Hold F or BOOST to use your boost reserve. Jammer fires at the nearest eligible rival ahead; it leaves their steering and flapping available. After a jam, that rival is immune to further jams for 10 seconds. Jammer is unavailable in practice.

    Multiplayer rooms

    Play online
    Join a public race. It starts automatically when at least two players are ready.
    Create room
    Create a private room and share its six-character code. The host chooses the course. Everyone selects Ready up; the host starts the race.
    Join room
    Enter a room code, choose your name and color, then select Ready up.
    Race again
    Return to the lobby after results. The host can select another course.

    No account is required. If the server is full or starting up, practice remains available. The race continues while your settings menu is open; the shared halfway shop pauses it for everyone.

    Settings

    Visual quality
    Adaptive adjusts resolution to performance. High adds stronger anti-aliasing. Low reduces rendering cost.
    Reduced camera motion
    Reduces chase-camera banking and animated effects.
    Audio
    Music and sound effects have separate volume controls.
    Personal bests
    Stored in your browser for each course and game version. Practice and multiplayer records are separate.
